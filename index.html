POSTPARTUM CARE

When to Start

The right time depends on your birth, healing and symptoms. Your first step can be a conversation.

  • Early in recovery

    Gentle movement can begin when comfortable, with advice suited to your birth.

  • Before higher-impact exercise

    Review your readiness before running, jumping or heavier workouts.

  • If symptoms affect you

    Ask about leakage, pelvic heaviness or persistent discomfort.
